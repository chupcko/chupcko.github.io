Divider box

The finger-joint box (open top) with a grid of full-height dividers. The box panels use the chosen layout — in the joined ones neighboring panels share their joint edge, so the laser cuts both profiles in a single pass; the dividers cannot share edges and sit below the box block. Compartments are equal, or set the ratios field — positive numbers separated by commas, scaled to fill the box (so "1,2,1" and "30,60,30" mean the same). Dividers are flush with the box top, or give a lower divider height. They stand on the bottom; their end tabs go through the holes in the walls, and crossings interlock with half-lap slots (lengthwise dividers slotted from the top, widthwise from the bottom). Turn on joint numbers to engrave assembly hints — the same number marks the two parts of one joint: 1–8 on the box edges, 9 and up pairing each divider end with its wall holes.
