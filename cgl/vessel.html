Round vessel

Three parts: the wall strip (top, with a row of tabs on both its top and bottom edges), and the bottom disk + top frame ring (below it, each with a matching ring of slots). The wall rolls into a cylinder along its bend pattern, its tabs drop into the slots to locate and hold it — no partial-depth engraving, everything is a single through-cut — and its two straight ends are glued in a plain butt seam.
